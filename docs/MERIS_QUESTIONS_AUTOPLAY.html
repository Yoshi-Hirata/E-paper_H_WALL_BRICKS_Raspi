1. New plan: write every picture before the show, send only triggers during it / 新方案：演出前写完所有画面，演出中只发触发命令

EN

Since all 20 slots are identical and persistent, we will write every picture of the show into its own slot before the show (during our "Upload" step, with time to re-send to any board that did not ACK), and during the show the host sends only one broadcast 0x1D <slot> per picture. No 0x13 is sent while the show runs.

  • Slot 0 = the white standby picture (so the master's one-time power-up autoplay of slot 0 shows white).
  • Slots 1–19 = the pictures of the show for that bus, in order (19 pictures per garment per show is enough for us).
  • One broadcast 0x17 after power-up, then only 0x1D.
中文

既然 20 个槽位完全相同且持久保存，我们将在演出前把演出的每一幅画面各写入一个槽位（在我们的“Upload”步骤中完成，并留出时间向未 ACK 的板补发），演出中主机每幅画面只发一条广播 0x1D <槽位>。演出进行中不再发送任何 0x13。

  • 槽位 0 = 白色待机画面（主机板上电时对槽位 0 的一次性自动播放因此显示白色）。
  • 槽位 1–19 = 该总线在本场演出中的画面，按顺序排列（每件服装每场 19 幅对我们足够）。
  • 上电后广播一次 0x17，之后只发 0x1D。
Q1 — Anything against this? / 对此有无异议？
Do you see any problem with holding 19 show pictures + the white picture in slots 0–19 of every board for the whole evening, and with a show that consists only of 0x1D broadcasts 8 s apart (7 s refresh + 1 s)? Any state the board keeps between 0x1D commands that we should reset?
整晚在每块板的槽位 0–19 中保存 19 幅演出画面 + 白色画面，并以仅由间隔 8 秒（7 秒刷新 + 1 秒）的 0x1D 广播构成的演出方式运行，贵司是否认为有任何问题？板卡在两次 0x1D 之间是否保留任何需要我们复位的状态？

2. Autonomous playback if the host is lost / 主机失效时的自主播放

EN

Our second idea is a safety net: if a host (Radxa) fails during the show, the boards of that bus should be able to continue the sequence by themselves. Your reply mentioned that during 0x16 loop playback the master (address 0x01) advances the slaves with 0x1E, and 0x1B carries a start delay and a switch delay per slot. We would like to understand whether this can play a timed sequence — pictures at, for example, 0:00, 0:30, 1:00, 3:00, 3:45 — rather than a fixed interval.

中文

第二个想法是安全网：若演出中某台主机（Radxa）失效，该总线上的板卡应能自行继续播放序列。贵司回复中提到，在 0x16 循环播放期间主机板（地址 0x01）用 0x1E 推进从机，且 0x1B 为每个槽位携带开始延迟和切换延迟。我们想了解这是否能播放一个按时刻编排的序列——例如画面出现在 0:00、0:30、1:00、3:00、3:45——而不是固定间隔。

Q2 — Per-slot delays in 0x1B / 0x1B 的逐槽延迟.
For the start_delay and switch_delay fields (2 bytes each): what is the unit (seconds? 10 ms? 100 ms?), the maximum, and the exact meaning — "wait this long after the previous picture is complete before showing this slot", or "after the previous 0x1D was issued"? Are they used in single-shot mode (0x1D) at all, or only in 0x16 playback?
关于 start_delay 与 switch_delay 字段（各 2 字节）：单位是什么（秒？10 ms？100 ms？），最大值是多少，确切含义是什么——“上一幅画面完成后等待该时长再显示本槽”，还是“上一条 0x1D 发出后计时”？它们在单张模式（0x1D）中是否起作用，还是仅用于 0x16 播放？
Q3 — 0x16 loop playback as a timed sequence / 用 0x16 循环播放实现按时序列.
If we set a different switch_delay for each of slots 1..19 and start 0x16 from slot 1: will the master show slot 1, wait slot 1's delay, advance everyone to slot 2 (via 0x1E), and so on? Does the sequence stop after the last configured slot, or loop back? Can the sequence be stopped with 0x17 and restarted from an arbitrary slot?
若我们为槽位 1..19 分别设置不同的 switch_delay 并从槽位 1 启动 0x16：主机板会先显示槽位 1，等待槽位 1 的延迟，然后（通过 0x1E）把所有板推进到槽位 2，依此类推吗？序列在最后一个已配置的槽位后停止，还是循环回到开头？能否用 0x17 停止序列并从任意槽位重新开始？
Q4 — Timing accuracy / 时间精度.
How accurate is the master's timing over a 10-minute sequence (crystal tolerance, drift in seconds)? We have 10 buses (10 masters) that must stay within about ±0.3 s of each other for the whole show. Is the delay counted from the end of the refresh (which varies by a few hundred ms per board) or from a fixed clock?
主机板在 10 分钟序列中的计时精度如何（晶振公差、以秒计的漂移）？我们有 10 条总线（10 块主机板），整场演出需保持在约 ±0.3 秒之内。延迟是从刷新结束（每板相差几百毫秒）开始计，还是按固定时钟计？
Q5 — Slaves that miss 0x1E / 漏收 0x1E 的从机.
If a slave does not receive an 0x1E (bus noise), does it catch up at the next one (show the slot named in data[0]), or does it fall one step behind permanently? Does the master repeat 0x1E?
若某从机漏收一条 0x1E（总线噪声），它会在下一条时追上（显示 data[0] 指定的槽位），还是永久落后一步？主机板是否会重复发送 0x1E？
Q6 — Starting 10 buses together / 同时启动 10 条总线.
Each of our 10 hosts would send 0x16 to its own bus at the same instant (our hosts are synchronised to a few ms). Is the delay between receiving 0x16 and the first refresh deterministic (so that all 10 buses start within a few tens of ms)?
我们的 10 台主机会在同一时刻各自向本总线发送 0x16（主机之间同步到几毫秒）。从收到 0x16 到第一次刷新的延迟是否确定（使 10 条总线在几十毫秒内同时开始）？
Q7 — Host and boards at the same time / 主机与板卡同时驱动.
Could both run at once — the master's sequence as the safety net and our host still sending 0x1D <slot> at the same moments? If the host's 0x1D arrives while the master's sequence is running, what happens (ignored, extra refresh, sequence reset)? If this is not sensible, we will choose one mode per show.
两者能否同时运行——主机板的序列作为安全网，同时我们的主机仍在相同时刻发送 0x1D <槽位>？若主机的 0x1D 在主机板序列运行期间到达，会怎样（忽略、多刷一次、序列复位）？如果这样不合理，我们将每场演出只选一种模式。
Q8 — Power cycle / 断电重启.
If a bus loses power mid-sequence and comes back, does the master resume the 0x16 sequence by itself, or wait for a command? (We would prefer: wait, and show slot 0 = white.)
若某条总线在序列进行中断电并恢复，主机板会自行恢复 0x16 序列，还是等待命令？（我们倾向于：等待，并显示槽位 0 = 白色。）
